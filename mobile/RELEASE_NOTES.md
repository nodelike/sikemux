# 0.2.0

The phone app for Sikemux 0.6.1 and newer on your host.

• iPhone: the app is native Swift now. It opens faster, scrolls long chats smoothly and downloads at about a quarter of its old size.
• Sikemux speaks eight languages on iPhone and Android.
• Search a host's chats by what was said in them, and open one at those words.
• A decision an agent made shows under its reply, with Overrule.
• An agent waiting on you shows as waiting, whatever it is waiting for.
