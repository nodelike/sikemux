# 0.1.0

The first release of Sikemux for your phone: your coding agents, wherever you are.

• See every host signed in to your account and the agents running on it.
• Follow chats as they stream: tool rows, diffs, markdown and subagent cards.
• Send messages to your agents or start a new chat.
• Attach photos from the camera, your library or Files.
• Get a notification when an agent finishes or needs you.
