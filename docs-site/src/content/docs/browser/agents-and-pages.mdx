---
title: How agents use pages
description: "What an agent can do with a tab, and pages that need a password."
---

An agent drives its desk tabs through Sikemux's `browser` tool. It can:

- read a page as numbered elements and text, or just the part around a phrase or at its end;
- click, type, press keys, scroll and drag;
- wait for a page to reach a state, such as text appearing, a streamed answer settling or the network going quiet;
- take screenshots, draw on them and record the tab;
- read the network calls and console messages of the page.

You see each of these happen on the desk.

## Pages that need a password

An agent never asks you to paste a password into its chat. When it needs one, it asks a **secret question**. You type or paste into a field that hides your text and keeps its line breaks, your answer goes straight to the agent as a file only your account can read, and the transcript shows only that it was answered.

Fields inside a frame from another site, such as a card field or a sign-in widget, can't be read by the agent. It can still click into them and type at the caret.
