---
title: How agents use pages
description: "What an agent can do with a tab, the artifacts it makes for you, and pages that need a password."
---

An agent drives its desk tabs through Sikemux's `browser_*` tools. It can:

- read a page as numbered elements and text, or just the part around a phrase or at its end;
- click, type, press keys, scroll and drag;
- run a script in the page;
- wait for a page to reach a state, such as text appearing, a streamed answer settling, the network going quiet or a script's check coming true;
- take screenshots, draw on them and record the tab;
- read the network calls and console messages of the page.

You see each of these happen on the desk.

## Artifacts

When an agent has something to show you, such as a report, a comparison or a mockup, it writes it as a web page. It checks the page off screen first with `page_preview`, then shows it with `page_show`, and its reply carries a card with the top of the page and its title; click the card to read it. A page loads the pictures, frames, stylesheets and scripts it names by a relative address from the folder it was written in, unless that folder is your home folder, Downloads or the like, and holds up to 25 MiB.

**Artifacts** on the tab rail (`⌘⌥8`, or `Ctrl+Alt+8` on Linux and Windows), beside **Agents**, **Terminals** and **Files**, lists what agents showed in the project, one line each with its title and the time it has left. Search finds one by title or by its agent. They sit in **Shared**, **Expiring** and **Kept**. The view in the middle shows the one you pick, or the newest.

- **Keep** holds an artifact until you let it go. One you don't keep goes 7 days after it was shown, or a day after its ask settles. An agent can show one already kept.
- **Share** makes a link, for 1 hour to 30 days, that anyone with it can open or only people signed in to Sikemux, at `app.sikemux.com/p/`. Sharing keeps the artifact, and the link shows its latest version.

Showing the same title again makes a new version. Ask an agent for one you saw before and it finds it with `page_list` and shows it again rather than making it anew.

On a Mac, pinch or double-tap with two fingers to zoom an artifact or an HTML file's preview, and move around it with two fingers. The next one opens at its own size.

## Pages that need a password

An agent never asks you to paste a password into its chat. When it needs one, it asks a **secret question**. You type or paste into a field that hides your text and keeps its line breaks, your answer goes straight to the agent as a file only your account can read, and the transcript shows only that it was answered.

Fields inside a frame from another site, such as a card field or a sign-in widget, can't be read by the agent. It can still click into them and type at the caret.
