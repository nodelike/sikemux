description: "Sikemux on iPhone and Android: your account's devices, the lock, and adding a host with a code."

Sikemux on your phone signs in to the same account as your Mac and reaches every host on it.

## Your account is the key

Every device and host signed in to your account reaches every host on it with full access, with nothing to approve: signing in is the proof. When a new device or host joins your account, each of your other devices says so, **New device on your account**, with its name; **Remove** takes it off your account and off every host at once.

A host you want to guard more can ask first instead: turn on **Require approval for new devices** for it, in the **⋯** beside it under **Hosts** in the app, in **Settings → Devices**, or at the top of its **Paired devices** on your phone. A new device or host then waits there, and your phone gets a notification to allow it, for ten minutes, even if the device gives up waiting sooner.

## The lock

Sikemux can ask for Face ID, Touch ID or your phone's passcode each time it opens, before it shows any host or chat. It is off until you turn on **Lock Sikemux** in the account sheet, which asks once to check it works; your phone needs a passcode for it.

With the lock on, Sikemux asks again once the app has been in the background for a minute, and while it is away the app switcher shows a blank screen in its place. A link or a notification you tap opens after you unlock. On iPhone, **Allow** or **Reject** on a notification is sent only then; on Android they answer from the notification itself, and **Allow** first asks for the phone's own lock. Signing out turns the lock off.

## Adding a host with a code

A server or spare computer with no browser signs in with `sikemux login`, which prints a code. To add it, open **Devices → Have a code?** on your phone, or app.sikemux.com/host in a browser, and type the code yourself. A link never fills it in, so nobody can send you one that adds their computer.

Before you add it, Sikemux shows when the host asked and where from: the country and network its address belongs to, then the address, or that the place is unknown. A host once removed from your account says when it was removed. Add only a computer you are setting up right now; **Not mine** stops its sign-in.

Adding a host needs a sign-in from the last ten minutes. If yours is older, Sikemux asks for your password, or a code sent to your email, and then adds the host.
