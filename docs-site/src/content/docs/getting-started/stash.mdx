---
title: Sharing and the Stash
description: "Send links, text, pictures and files from any app on your phone to a project's chief, or keep them in your Stash for later."
---

Sikemux is in your phone's share menu. Share a link, some text, a picture, a video or a file from any app, then pick a project's chief for it, or keep it in your **Stash**.

## Sharing to a chief

The share sheet shows what you are sharing, a note you can add, your **Project chiefs** and, fixed at the foot, **Stash**. Each chief is listed by its project's name, the last project you opened first, with its host where two share a name. Pick one and what you shared lands in that chief's message box with your note, ready for you to send. It is never sent by itself. A chief that is asleep is woken, and a closed one is opened again.

The host the chief's chat runs on puts it there as soon as it hears about it. A host that is asleep or offline does it the next time it connects to your account.

If its project is no longer open on that host, what you shared stays in your Stash.

## The Stash

The Stash keeps what you shared for later. It belongs to your account, so every device and host signed in to it sees the same Stash.

On the Mac, **Open Stash** in a chat's Stash tray opens the whole Stash in a tab. Each item shows as a card: a link with its page's picture, title and site, a photo, the start of a text, or a file. **Add to chat** puts it in the chat you were last writing in, and its arrow, or **Add to chat** when no chat is open, picks another. **Open** opens a link in your browser or a file in its app, and copies a text; the bin removes the item. You can also drag a card onto any chat.

In a chat's message box, on the Mac and on your phone, **Stash** beside **+** opens your Stash, newest first, with what arrived since you last looked marked **New** and counted on the button. **Add N new** adds everything new. On the Mac it opens as a tray above the message box, one row per item: its first photo or its page's picture, its words with the agents, files, pull requests and issues they name drawn as they are in a chat, the files and other links it carries, and how long ago you stashed it. Click an item, or move with the arrow keys and press Return, to add it, and **Open Stash** opens the whole Stash. Typing `&` opens the same tray and narrows it by title, note, link or text as you type. Opening the Stash counts as seeing what is new, on every device.

What you add sits in the message box as a small preview: a picture or file as its thumbnail, copied to the host the chat runs on, and a link or text with its site's icon and title, which goes to the agent with the message when you send it. Your note goes into what you are writing.

### Stashing a prompt

You can stash a whole message to send later: press `⌘S` in the message box on the Mac, or hold the send button on your phone, or pick **Stash this prompt** in the Stash. Its words and files become one item and the message box empties; **Undo** brings it back for a few seconds. A prompt shows in the Stash with its first lines and its files, and the pages it links to show their titles and pictures once Sikemux has read them. Picking it later puts its words and files back in a message box, on any of your devices.

An item you have used leaves the Stash and is deleted 30 days later. One item can be up to 25 MB, and your Stash can hold up to 1 GB.

## Who can see it

Your Stash is kept on Sikemux's account server, encrypted at rest with a key the server holds. It is not end-to-end encrypted: being signed in to your account is all the access it needs, and nobody else's account can reach it. Agents see an item only once you attach it to a message.
