---
title: Languages
description: "Sikemux is in English for now."
---

Sikemux's own words are in English on every computer and phone, whatever your system's language. What your agents write, and your files and terminals, stay as they are, so an agent still answers in the language you write to it.

The earlier translations were machine-made and nobody had checked them, so they are gone until a native speaker reviews one. If you'd like to review Sikemux in your language, tell us.
