Sikemux speaks English, Español, 简体中文, Deutsch, Français, 日本語, 한국어, Русский and Português (Brasil). It starts in your system's language when it speaks it, and in English otherwise. What your agents write, and your files and terminals, stay as they are.

The Mac, iPhone and Android apps are translated throughout. The translations are machine-made for now; tell us where one reads wrong.

- **Mac:** **Settings → Interface → Language**. The window changes at once.
- **iPhone:** **Language** in the account sheet opens Sikemux's page in iOS Settings, where iOS keeps each app's language.
- **Android:** **Language** in the account sheet. Pick **Same as the system** to follow your phone.
