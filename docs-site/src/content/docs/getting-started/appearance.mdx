## Theme

**Settings → Appearance → Theme** picks the colours Sikemux wears: one of its own themes, one of Ghostty's, or one you make. Dark and light themes are both there.

## Style

**Settings → Appearance → Interface → Style** sets how the window is drawn, in whatever theme you wear:

- **Soft**, the default, rounds every corner and floats the rails and the view in front apart, with soft shadows under what floats.
- **Sharp** squares every corner and sets the rails and the view in front flush, parted by hairlines, as sikemux.com is drawn. What floats, such as the message box and menus, casts a hard shadow: the accent on a dark theme, the ink on a light one, whose hairlines also take the full ink. Small accent squares mark where the rails' hairlines meet the top and bottom of the window, and group labels such as PROJECTS are set in the mono face.

The change shows at once, in every window.

## Text

**Text size** scales the interface's labels and controls to 100%, 110% or 125%. **Chat width** sets how wide a chat's messages grow on a wide window; see [Chats](/agents/chats).
