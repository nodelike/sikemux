A goal is a card on the [agent board](/agents/board/) with slices under it. **Goals** shows each goal and its slices, and puts what needs you first. Open it from a project in the side rail, or press `⌘⌥7`.

## What needs you

Goals opens on what needs you in the project and what is moving. **Needs you** lists what waits on you, grouped by what you're needed for: **Decide** (a gate to pass, a question, a permission or a pull request), **Unblock** (a sign-in, a secret, a terminal waiting for you to type) and **Fix** (a failed check or something held). Each line names its goal or agent and how long it has waited, and opens the slice or the agent's chat. **Moving** lists the goals with work under way, each with a strip of its slices, and **Done** the goals whose slices have all passed. Click a goal to open it.

When things finished or failed while you were away, the line under the title says how many, and opens **Overnight**: how many finished, failed and wait on you, then each event in time order, grouped by goal. Each event opens its slice or its agent's chat.

## A goal

The goal shows who leads it and which agents own its slices, a strip with one segment per slice, and **Next**: the gates its slices wait at, in the order they will be reached. **Needs you** lists the slices waiting at a gate only you pass. **Slices** lists every slice with what it waits on, its ticket and its owner.

**Rules** lists the rules the goal's agents follow: the goal's own, then those Sikemux keeps for the project and the folders above it ([what Sikemux remembers](/agents/memory/)). **Move to AGENTS.md** moves a rule kept for the project into its repository's `AGENTS.md`, where every agent reads it with the code, and Sikemux stops keeping it. The file is left uncommitted for you to review. Sensitive rules never go into the repository, and if a rule can't move, Sikemux says why.

Every slice wears the same mark: solid green when done, a green ring while it moves, a dashed grey ring while it waits, amber when it needs you and red when its latest check failed.

## A slice

Click a slice to open it. Under its promise and owner, the ladder shows each gate with who passed it and when, or who may pass it and what it still needs.

The evidence for the gate waiting leads, large, a picture first when there is one; the rest sit as thumbnails under it, and clicking one brings it to the front. Clicking a picture or recording opens it in the viewer. The checks Sikemux ran fold into one line.

When the slice waits on you, **Looks right** passes the gate. **Needs work** opens a line to say what is wrong; the note is pinned to the evidence in front and goes to the agent whose work it shows. Notes are listed under the buttons, newest first.
