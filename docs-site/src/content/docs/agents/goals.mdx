A goal is a card at the top of the [agent board](/agents/board/) with slices under it. A slice at the top, with no goal above it, such as a card Auto runs, is a goal of its own with one slice. **Goals** is a view of its own that takes the whole window: it puts what needs you first, then each goal and its slices, across every project. Switch to it with the goals button at the top right of the window, beside Run, or press `⌘⌥7`; press either again to go back to the workbench. The window keeps whichever you left it on.

The goals view has a rail of its own: **Home** with how many things need you, **Overnight** with how many happened while you were away, every project's goals under the project's name, each with the mark of where it stands, and your hosts at the bottom with how many agents each runs.

## Starting work

The box at the top of Home asks what should get done, in the project in front of the workbench. Pick how it starts: **Do it** adds a card and starts an agent on it, **Plan first** adds a goal and starts a lead that talks it through with you and proposes slices for you to approve, and **With me** starts a chat with you, kept as a goal. While you type, Sikemux picks one for you and says why, such as when the text touches server code or a path your project lists under [`planFirst`](/getting-started/projects/); you can always pick another. **Start** or Enter starts it with the agent the rail starts with. That agent holds its card from the start.

## What needs you

Goals opens on **Home**: what needs you and what is moving, across every project. **Needs you** lists what waits on you, grouped by what you're needed for: **Decide** (a gate to pass, a question, a permission or a pull request), **Unblock** (a sign-in, a secret, a terminal waiting for you to type) and **Fix** (a failed check or something held). Each line names its goal or agent and how long it has waited, and opens the slice or the agent's chat. **Moving** lists the goals with work under way, each with its project and a strip of its slices, and **Done** the goals whose slices have all passed. Click a goal to open it.

Whatever comes to need you also tells you, once: a notification on your Mac while Sikemux isn't in front, and on your phone as its notification settings say. That covers a gate to pass, a card handed back, agents stuck on each other, knowledge that no longer holds and a turn that ends asking you something, besides the permissions, questions and problems that always did. It doesn't tell you again about the same thing, or while you have that agent open, and the notification goes away once it no longer needs you.

When things finished or failed while you were away, the line under the title says how many, and opens **Overnight**: how many finished, failed and wait on you, then each event in time order, grouped by goal. Each event opens its slice or its agent's chat.

## A goal

The goal shows who leads it and which agents own its slices, a strip with one segment per slice, and **Next**: the gates its slices wait at, in the order they will be reached. **Needs you** lists the slices waiting at a gate only you pass. **Slices** lists every slice with what it waits on, its ticket and its owner.

**Assertions** lists what must hold once the goal is done, each with the kind of evidence that shows it (the check passing, a recording, a screenshot, or a CI workflow passing, named on the line as "CI Build passes") and how many of the slices that fulfil it have that evidence. Add one on the line under them, picking its kind; **CI workflow** asks for the workflow's name. Retire one with **×**.

**Slices land** sets who passes Landed in the project: **Once on the branch**, which Sikemux passes, or **When you pass it**, where nothing counts as landed until you pass Landed yourself ([when slices land](/agents/board/#when-slices-land)).

**Rules** lists the rules the goal's agents follow: the goal's own, then those Sikemux keeps for the project and the folders above it ([what Sikemux remembers](/agents/memory/)). **Move to AGENTS.md** moves a rule kept for the project into its repository's `AGENTS.md`, where every agent reads it with the code, and Sikemux stops keeping it. The file is left uncommitted for you to review. The button shows only on a rule that can move: never a sensitive one, which never goes into the repository, nor one kept for a folder that isn't the top of a git checkout, or whose `AGENTS.md` links outside it.

Every slice wears the same mark: solid green when done, a green ring while it moves, a dashed grey ring while it waits, amber when it needs you and red when its latest check failed. A goal wears the mark of its slices together: red when one failed, amber when one needs you, a green ring when one moves, solid green when every one is done, and a dashed grey ring otherwise.

## What a slice waits on

You never have to ask an agent for its status. Next and each slice's row say what the slice waits on now, and roughly how long that takes:

- **the project's check** on its owner's latest commit, here or in CI: queued, then running, such as "check · ~3 min" or "CI Release · ~6 min"
- **another card** it waits on, such as "waits for #4"
- **you**, at a gate only you pass
- **its owner**, in the middle of a turn

A check's estimate comes from how long the project's last few checks took since Sikemux last started; there is none until one has run. A wait that runs past its estimate says "over" instead, such as "check · over 3 min". The pane changes the moment a wait starts, ends or runs over.

## A slice

Click a slice to open it. Under its promise and owner, the ladder shows each gate with who passed it and when, or who may pass it and what it still needs. Under the ladder, each of the goal's assertions the slice fulfils is ticked once its evidence held up; an assertion it named that no longer stands on its goal, such as one retired, shows struck through as freed. Each thumbnail of evidence says which assertions it was collected for, by number.

The evidence for the gate waiting leads, large, a picture first when there is one; the rest sit as thumbnails under it, and clicking one brings it to the front. Clicking a picture or recording opens it in the viewer. The checks Sikemux ran fold into one line.

When the slice waits on you, **Looks right** passes the gate. **Needs work** opens a line to say what is wrong; the note is pinned to the evidence in front and goes to the agent whose work it shows. Notes are listed under the buttons, newest first.
