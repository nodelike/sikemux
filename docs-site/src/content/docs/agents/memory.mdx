---
title: What Sikemux remembers
description: "Rules, knowledge and decisions you say once, which the agents started in the project are handed."
---

Sikemux keeps what you would otherwise repeat in every chat, so you say it once and the agents started in the project are handed it. It keeps three kinds of thing, each at one level:

| Kind          | Kept for                          | Example                                | Kept until                                 |
| ------------- | --------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Rules**     | The project, or one goal          | "Shadows, never glows"                 | Someone retires it, or its goal is removed |
| **Knowledge** | The project, or a folder above it | "The service is called ai-service now" | It is proven wrong                         |
| **Decisions** | A goal, on its card               | "One project is one concept"           | Someone retires it, or the goal is removed |

Rules and knowledge kept for a folder above the project apply to every project under it, for a product that spans several repositories.

Agents keep rules and knowledge with the `memory_add`, `memory_update` and `memory_list` tools, and a goal's decisions with `board_decide`, when you tell them to remember something or when they find it out. Each one says which agent learned it, in which chat and when, so you can check where it came from. A correction keeps what it said before. A goal's decisions and its own rules show on its card on the board; a goal keeps 64 of each, and past that drops its oldest retired ones first. A goal is a card at the top of the board, and once it has decisions or rules of its own it can't be moved under another card.

## What an agent is handed

When another agent or Auto starts an agent in a project, its task ends with what Sikemux keeps, in the same message:

- every rule of the project and of the folders above it
- only the knowledge its task names: knowledge that names paths or services goes to agents whose task or card names one of them as a whole word, and knowledge that names none goes to every agent. For Auto's agents only the card counts, not Auto's own instructions.
- once the agent holds a card, the rules and decisions of the goal the card sits under. An agent started by another agent usually claims its card after it starts, so it reads those with `memory_list` then; Auto's agents hold their card from the start.

A chat you start yourself is handed what Sikemux keeps once, as it starts: every rule of the project and of the folders above it, and the knowledge that names nothing in particular. Claude Code and Codex take it among their instructions, after any developer instructions of your own in Codex's config; other agents get it with your first message that isn't a slash command, shown there as context rather than as your words. It holds what Sikemux kept when the chat began; what changes later, and a goal's rules and decisions once the agent holds a card, it reads with `memory_list`.

A reviewer Auto starts to judge a change blind is handed only what you said yourself, never what an agent added.

What Sikemux keeps stays out of the agent CLIs' own memory, so it is the same for every agent, account and CLI.

## Rules from your past sessions

The first time you start a goal in a project, Sikemux looks through what you typed in that project's past sessions with Claude Code, Codex, Pi, OMP, Grok, Hermes and OpenCode for the rules you keep repeating. It reads only your own messages: never what an agent wrote, its tools' output, or messages from Sikemux and other agents. It leaves out anything that looks pasted, such as a log or a long spec, and anything that looks like a secret, such as a key, a token, a password or an environment file's line, so none of it leaves Sikemux. It then asks Claude Code once, or Codex when Claude Code isn't installed, to find rules said in at least two sessions. That asking starts no chat and saves no session.

What it finds is offered on the goals view's Home and in a goal's **Rules**, and in the inbox as something to decide: "You've said these rules in past sessions. Keep them?" Each rule is ticked; untick any you don't want, then **Keep** makes the ticked ones rules of the project, as yours, and **Dismiss** keeps none. It runs once per project, even after Sikemux restarts; **Find rules in past sessions** under a goal's rules runs it again. Phones and the notch show what it found but can't keep it yet.

## Sensitive entries

A rule or knowledge marked sensitive, such as a host or an address, never goes into the repository. Agents in the project are still handed it, since that is what it is for, so it shows in their chats. Where Sikemux lists what it keeps, only its own window shows sensitive entries; phones and other programs see how many were left out, not what they say, and the inbox says only that sensitive knowledge no longer holds.

## When knowledge no longer holds

Knowledge can be proven wrong two ways.

- **Someone says so.** You, or an agent that runs into the opposite, either corrects it with what is so instead, or says it no longer holds without knowing what does.
- **Sikemux tests it.** Knowledge can say how to test it: a path exists, or a host answers on a port. Sikemux tests it every six hours, and soon after an agent was handed it on a test more than ten minutes old. An agent never waits for a test; it is handed what the last one found. A host that can't be reached at all, such as an internal one while the VPN is off, proves nothing, so the knowledge stays as it was; only a host that refuses the connection counts against it.

Knowledge that failed its test, or that someone said no longer holds, is left out of what agents are handed and shows in the inbox as something to fix. Correct it or retire it. Knowledge that only failed its test holds again by itself once the test passes.
