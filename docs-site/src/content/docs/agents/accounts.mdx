---
title: Accounts and limits
description: "Several Claude or Codex accounts, and how much of a plan is left."
---

Claude Code and Codex can keep several accounts, such as one for work and one for yourself. Each account is a separate folder for the CLI's own sign-in. Sikemux never sees or stores the credentials.

## Add an account

In **Settings → Accounts**, under Claude or Codex, choose **Add account**, give it a name, then sign in in your browser. Once it is signed in, **Switch to** it, or **Keep using** the account you had. Each account in the list has **Use** to switch to it, which moves your open chats there too, and its **⋯** menu signs it out or removes it.

With two or more accounts, **Auto-rotate at limits** lets a chat that hits a usage limit carry on with your next signed-in account. It is off by default: each provider's terms expect one person's ordinary use per plan.

## Choose a chat's account

With two or more signed-in accounts, the strip above a chat's composer shows the chat's account at its right end. Pick another there: a new or sleeping chat starts on it when you begin writing, and a chat already going moves to it, carrying on any turn in progress.

## Limits

The foot of the tab rail's agents page shows each agent's weekly limit, for Claude and Codex together, or just the agent chosen in the rail. Each row is the agent's mark, a ruler and what is left of the week added up across its accounts: `84% / 200%` is 84 points left out of two accounts' 100 each. The ruler fills with what is left and turns amber, then red, as it runs low.

Rest the pointer on the rows and a float opens with one line per account: its ruler, how much of its week is left and the countdown to its reset, with the account in use lifted. **Move chats here** on another account puts the agent on it and moves your open chats there too; **Undo**, for a few seconds, puts each chat back on the account it was on. Chats running in a terminal stay where they are. With every agent listed, each has a **+** in the float to add an account; with one agent, the **+** is beside the refresh button. A signed-out account offers **Sign in**.

The chip beside the Goals button in the top bar shows each agent's week left, added up the same way; click it for the same rows and accounts in a popover, without having to hover. Sikemux reads your limits when it starts, every five minutes while the panel or the popover is on screen, and when you open the popover on a reading older than that; the refresh button reads them again now. A failed read keeps the last numbers and says how old they are.

An account you signed out of waits under **Add account**, where you can sign in to it again or remove it.

## Agent profiles

**Agent profiles**, at the foot of **Settings → Accounts**, choose which copy of an agent a launch uses: give a profile a name, the agent, and the path to its program, or leave the path empty to use the one on your `PATH`. A profile can also name its own folder, for keeping several accounts side by side. Profiles never hold credentials.
